### 1. Comparative Endophthalmitis Rates of Anti-VEGF, Corticosteroid Implants and Anti-Complement Injections: An IRIS Registry Analysis.
- Authors: Yi Stephanie Zhang et al. | Date: 2026-Nov | PMID: 42472569 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42472569/) | DOI: 10.1016/j.ajo.2026.07.030

**Abstract (EN)**

To evaluate the incidence of endophthalmitis after intravitreal injections using a large, national ophthalmic registry.
Retrospective clinical cohort study.
Patients in the American Academy of Ophthalmology IRIS Registry (Intelligent Research in Sight) who received intravitreal injections of anti-vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) agents, fluocinolone acetonide (Iluvien, Yutiq), avacincaptad pegol (Izervay), pegcetacoplan (Syfovre), or dexamethasone (Ozurdex) were included in this analysis. Data from pediatric and adult patients in the IRIS Registry who were diagnosed with acute endophthalmitis within 30 days after intravitreal injections from 2016 to 2024 were collected.
The overall incidence of endophthalmitis in adults was 3.5 per 10,000 cases with rates of 3.3 per 10,000 after anti-VEGF, 4.9 per 10,000 after avacincaptad pegol, 6.6 per 10,000 after pegcetacoplan, 10 per 10,000 and 8 per 10,000 after fluocinolone acetonide (Iluvien and Yutiq, respectively), and 12 per 10,000 after dexamethasone implant. The rate of endophthalmitis was significantly higher for corticosteroid implants and pegcetacoplan compared to anti-VEGF injections. Over the past years, there were statistically significant yet small fluctuations in endophthalmitis incidence for anti-VEGF injections ranging from 2.4 per 10,000 in 2021 to 4 per 10,000 in 2020.
The incidence of post-injection endophthalmitis is greater with corticosteroid implants and anticomplement therapies. Heightened aseptic vigilance may be warranted with injections that necessitate larger gauge needles, modulate the immune response and/or are prepared from a vial.

To determine the incidence of poor responsiveness to VEGF-A suppression in eyes with diabetic macular edema (DME) by evaluating the effect of continuous delivery of ranibizumab via the Port Delivery Platform with ranibizumab (PDS) in individual patients enrolled in the Pagoda clinical trial.
Retrospective analysis of prospective clinical trial data SUBJECTS: DME patients enrolled in Pagoda (NCT04108156).

CST, BCVA, and time to resolution of edema.

### 4. Choroidal Thickness and Axial Length as Independent Correlates of AMD: Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey 2017-2020.
- Authors: Mihyun Choi et al. | Date: 2026-Oct-29 | PMID: 42957012 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42957012/) | DOI: 10.1016/j.ajo.2026.10.015

**Abstract (EN)**

To examine how choroidal thickness and axial length relate to age-related macular degeneration (AMD) across disease stages in a nationwide Korean population.
Cross-sectional, population-based study.
We analyzed the Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey, 2017-2020, including 15,388 adults aged 40 years or older with gradable fundus photographs; ocular analyses included 13,807 after excluding coexisting macular disease. Axial length (AL), central macular thickness (CMT), and subfoveal choroidal thickness (SFCT) were measured by partial coherence interferometry and optical coherence tomography. Associations were assessed by survey-weighted logistic regression adjusted for age, sex, and systemic factors.
Weighted AMD prevalence was 14.41% (95% CI, 13.67-15.15; early, 13.46%; late, 0.95%). In multivariable models, greater SFCT was associated with early AMD (OR per 10 µm, 1.03; 95% CI, 1.02-1.03; P < .001), and shorter AL with early AMD (OR per 1 mm, 0.76; 0.71-0.82; P < .001) and late AMD (0.74; 0.57-0.97; P = .030). Greater CMT was associated with late AMD (OR per 10 µm, 1.10; 1.01-1.19; P = .030) and neovascular AMD (1.23; 1.12-1.35; P < .001). SFCT and AL remained associated with early AMD after mutual adjustment (both P < .001). Older age, male sex, non-white-collar occupation, and rural residence were associated with AMD, and anemia with late AMD (1.78; 1.06-2.96; P = .028).
Greater SFCT and shorter AL were independently associated with early AMD. These findings suggest that choroidal thickness and axial length may reflect distinct structural correlates.

### 5. Diagnostic Accuracy of Paracentral Acute Middle Maculopathy for Arteritic Anterior Ischemic Optic Neuropathy.
- Authors: Sanjana Molleti et al. | Date: 2026-Oct-29 | PMID: 42957016 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42957016/) | DOI: 10.1016/j.ajo.2026.10.022

**Abstract (EN)**

The purpose of this study was to evaluate the diagnostic accuracy of paracentral acute middle maculopathy (PAMM) detected on optical coherence tomography (OCT) macula for differentiating arteritic anterior ischemic neuropathy (AAION) from nonarteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION) in a multicenter United States cohort.
Retrospective, multicenter diagnostic accuracy analysis.
This study included patients diagnosed with AAION or NAION at the University of Minnesota, Mayo Clinic, and Johns Hopkins Wilmer Eye Institute between January 2020 and January 2025. Inclusion criteria consisted of patients older than 50 years who had obtained OCT macula within 14 days of symptom onset. AAION diagnoses were confirmed by temporal artery biopsy or positron emission tomography/computed tomography.
Two masked fellowship-trained neuro-ophthalmologists independently evaluated OCT images for the presence of acute PAMM.
Sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value (PPV), and negative predictive value (NPV) of PAMM for AAION were calculated.
A total of 119 patients were included, consisting of 27 patients with AAION and 92 patients with NAION. PAMM was identified in 18.5% of patients with AAION and 5.4% of patients with NAION (p = 0.032). The sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV of PAMM for AAION was 19% [95% CI: 0.08-0.37], 94.6% [95% CI: 0.88-0.98], 50% [95% CI: 0.24-0.76], and79.8% [95% CI: 0.71-0.86] respectively. Three of five NAION patients with PAMM had provoked NAION associated with severe systemic hypoperfusion.
PAMM is a highly specific but insensitive OCT marker for AAION. Although its presence should increase clinical suspicion for AAION, PAMM may rarely occur in patients with NAION particularly in the setting of systemic hypoperfusion and should be interpreted within the overall clinical context.

### 6. Retinal phenotypes and longitudinal clinical courses in patients with NPHP1-associated Senior-Løken syndrome.
- Authors: Kazuki Kuniyoshi et al. | Date: 2026-Oct-29 | PMID: 42957013 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42957013/) | DOI: 10.1016/j.ajo.2026.10.020

**Abstract (EN)**

This study aimed to describe retinal phenotype, longitudinal clinical course, and genetic findings in Japanese patients with NPHP1-associated Senior-Løken syndrome and their siblings.
Retrospective, multicenter case series.
This study included 12 patients with biallelic pathogenic NPHP1 variants, comprising 10 probands with symptomatic retinal dystrophy recruited from six ophthalmic centers across Japan and 2 affected siblings identified through family screening. Clinical and genetic findings were analyzed. Retinal dystrophy was diagnosed using fundus photography, fundus autofluorescence (FAF), optical coherence tomography, and electroretinography. Genetic testing was performed using whole-exome and/or whole-genome sequencing. Follow-up ranged from 0 to 44 years (mean, 14.6 years).
Retinal phenotype, onset of retinopathy and nephropathy, and genotype.

- Authors: Hannah Jensen et al. | Date: 2026-Nov-01 | PMID: 41666364 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41666364/) | DOI: 10.1097/IAE.0000000000004788

**Abstract (EN)**

Investigate the structure-function relationships between epiretinal membrane (ERM) stage, tractional abnormalities, and the gap area between the ERM and the retinal surface, in relation to visual acuity and quantitative contrast sensitivity function in patients with idiopathic epiretinal membrane.
This was a retrospective, cross-sectional study involving 111 participants diagnosed with idiopathic ERM. All patients underwent a comprehensive ophthalmologic examination, spectral-domain optical coherence tomography imaging using the SPECTRALIS Heidelberg system, and contrast sensitivity (CS) testing using the quantitative contrast sensitivity function method.

**Abstract (EN)**

To quantify macular microvascular modifications and retinal fluorophores in early diabetic retinal disease (DRD) in type 1 diabetes mellitus (DM1), and to explore their interrelationship.

### 5. Retina Insurance Reimbursements of Pediatric Retina Patients in a Hospital versus Outpatient Surgical Setting.
- Authors: Ravneet Rai et al. | Date: 2026-Oct-27 | PMID: 42944618 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42944618/) | DOI: 10.1097/IAE.0000000000005040

**Abstract (EN)**

To evaluate reimbursement patterns for pediatric vitreoretinal procedures performed at a tertiary children's hospital versus an ambulatory surgery center (ASC).
A retrospective review was conducted of retinal procedures in pediatric patients performed between 1/2022 and 1/2025 at a tertiary children's hospital and an ambulatory surgery center in Texas. Self-pay visits and visits with zero billed charges were excluded. The primary outcome was total median reimbursement percentage based on physician-level services. Secondary outcomes included median reimbursement percentage by payer type, public insurer subgroup, surgical setting, and current procedural terminology (CPT) code. The proportion of visits with zero reimbursement was also evaluated.
394 visits from 282 patients were analyzed, including 219 visits at the children's hospital and 175 at the ASC. ### 6. Association of Retinal Microvascular Density with Hemodynamic and Clinical Severity Markers in Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension.
- Authors: Murat Erdağ et al. | Date: 2026-Oct-29 | PMID: 42954929 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42954929/) | DOI: 10.1097/IAE.0000000000005043

**Abstract (EN)**

To evaluate whether quantitative retinal microvascular and structural parameters, measured by optical coherence tomography angiography (OCTA), are associated with disease severity and right ventriculo-arterial coupling in patients with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH).
This cross-sectional study included 45 patients with confirmed PAH, stratified by New York Heart Association (NYHA) functional class (I-III). Patients with coexisting ocular pathologies were strictly excluded. Vessel density (VD) in the superficial (SCP) and deep capillary plexuses (DCP), foveal avascular zone characteristics, and ganglion cell complex (GCC) thickness were analyzed. These parameters were correlated with functional capacity (6-minute walk test [6-mWT]), NT-proBNP levels, and the echocardiographic TAPSE/sPAP ratio.
Retinal VD in both SCP and DCP progressively decreased with worsening NYHA functional class (p < 0.05). Retinal VD showed a significant negative correlation with NT-proBNP levels (r = -0.355, p = 0.017) and a positive correlation with 6-mWT distance (r = 0.499, p < 0.001). Crucially, retinal microvascular density demonstrated a robust positive correlation with the TAPSE/sPAP ratio (r = 0.420, p = 0.004), a surrogate for right ventriculo-arterial coupling. GCC thickness exhibited variations across NYHA classes but lacked a consistent linear progression.
Retinal microvascular attenuation in PAH is strongly associated not only with functional and biochemical severity but also with central hemodynamic impairment represented by the TAPSE/sPAP ratio. These findings suggest that OCTA-derived vessel density may serve as a promising, non-invasive biomarker for monitoring systemic microvascular compromise and right heart function in PAH.

### 7. Longitudinal Changes in Scleral Thickness after Half-dose Photodynamic Therapy in Central Serous Chorioretinopathy.
- Authors: Marina Maehira et al. | Date: 2026-Oct-29 | PMID: 42954920 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42954920/) | DOI: 10.1097/IAE.0000000000005042

**Abstract (EN)**

To evaluate longitudinal changes in scleral thickness after half-dose photodynamic therapy (PDT) in eyes with central serous chorioretinopathy (CSC) in parallel with widefield assessment of choroidal thickness.
This retrospective observational study included 41 eyes of 41 patients with unilateral CSC who underwent half-dose PDT for persistent subretinal fluid and completed 12 months of follow-up. Scleral thickness was measured in 4 quadrants using anterior-segment optical coherence tomography (OCT) at baseline and at 3 and 12 months after PDT. These findings suggest that scleral thickening in CSC is unlikely to represent a readily reversible response to treatment-associated choroidal changes and may instead reflect a relatively stable anatomical feature.

To evaluate the relationship between best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) and reading speed in the sham-treated cohorts from two Phase 3 clinical trials of revakinagene taroretcel-lwey for macular telangiectasia type 2 (MacTel) and to determine whether reading speed captures functional decline not reflected by changes in BCVA.

The denominator was the total number of anti-VEGF injections for nAMD during the same period. Incidence per 1,000 injections and site-adjusted incidence rate ratios (IRRs) were estimated using quasi-Poisson regression.
Among 47,358 injections, 54 SMH events requiring procedural intervention were identified (1.14 per 1,000 injections). Median final BCVA after procedural treatment was 1.00 logMAR (Snellen equivalent, 20/200), compared with 0.46 logMAR before SMH (approximately 20/60). PCV accounted for 39 events (72.2%), and prior SMH was documented in 12 eyes (22.2%). Crude incidence was 2.20 for ranibizumab, 1.09 for brolucizumab, 1.08 for aflibercept, and 0.24 for faricimab. Using aflibercept as the reference, IRRs were 1.83 for ranibizumab (95% confidence interval [CI], 0.73-4.59; P = 0.195), 0.91 for brolucizumab (95% CI, 0.25-3.28; P = 0.883), and 0.20 for faricimab (95% CI, 0.01-3.16; P = 0.251).
SMH requiring procedural intervention during anti-VEGF therapy for nAMD was uncommon but visually severe; among affected eyes, PCV accounted for 72.2% of events. Exploratory drug-specific analyses did not demonstrate significant adjusted differences among anti-VEGF agents. Future patient-level studies should evaluate subtype-specific incidence, hemorrhagic recurrence, and polyp-directed treatment strategies in PCV-rich populations.
